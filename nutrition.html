Eat well, feel nourished

Healthy nutrition

Good nutrition is about variety, balance and habits you can maintain—not strict rules or one “perfect” food.

The balanced plate

At meals, aim for a variety of foods. Vegetables and fruits can add colour and fibre; whole grains provide energy and fibre; and protein foods help build and maintain body tissues. The right balance can differ from person to person.

  • Include vegetables or fruit in meals and snacks.
  • Choose whole grains such as oats, brown rice or whole-wheat foods when available.
  • Include protein sources such as beans, lentils, eggs, fish, dairy, nuts or other suitable options.
  • Enjoy different foods and pay attention to hunger and fullness cues.

Make variety easy

Eating a range of foods across the week can make meals more interesting. Try seasonal produce, local ingredients and simple home-cooked combinations. Frozen and canned options can also be useful choices; compare labels where appropriate.

Try this today

Add one colourful fruit or vegetable that you do not usually eat to a meal.

Remember hydration

Needs vary with activity, climate, age and health. Keep water accessible and drink regularly, especially in hot weather or when you are active.

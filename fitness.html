Fitness & wellbeing

Being active does not have to mean intense workouts. Everyday movement, enough rest and caring for your mental wellbeing all contribute to a healthy lifestyle.

Make movement part of your day

Choose activities that suit your ability, interests and routine. You might walk with a friend, take the stairs when comfortable, cycle, dance, garden or play a sport. Start at a manageable level and build gradually.

  • Break up long periods of sitting with short movement breaks.
  • Try activities you enjoy so that you are more likely to continue.
  • Warm up, use suitable equipment and respect your body's limits.
  • Rest when you need to and seek professional guidance if exercise causes concerning symptoms.
  • Set daily goals: Aim for a realistic amount of physical activity each day and gradually increase it.
  • Stay active at home: Do simple exercises, stretching or household chores to keep your body moving.
  • Choose active transport: Walk or cycle for short journeys whenever possible and safe.
  • Stay hydrated: Drink enough water before, during and after physical activity.
  • Make it a habit: Schedule regular movement breaks and involve family or friends to stay motivated.

Rest and sleep matter

A regular wind-down routine can help prepare you for sleep. Try keeping a consistent bedtime, making your room comfortable and reducing stimulating screen use close to bedtime when possible. Sleep needs vary by age and individual.

Look after your mental wellbeing

Mental wellbeing is part of overall health. Make time for supportive relationships, hobbies, time outdoors and calming activities. Taking a few slow breaths or pausing between tasks can help you reset during a busy day.
